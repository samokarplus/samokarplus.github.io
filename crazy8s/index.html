Crazy Eights

The rules

One standard 52-card deck. Two players get seven cards each; three or four players get five.

Match the top card's rank or the active suit. An eight can be played on any card, and you choose the next suit.

If you cannot play, click the deck to draw one card at a time until you get a playable card, then play it. If the stock runs out, the discards are shuffled back in, keeping the top card. If no cards remain to draw, your turn passes.

First to empty their hand wins. If everyone is stuck, the lowest hand score wins: eights 50, face cards 10, aces 1, other cards their number. No stacking or special action cards.

The host keeps this tab open. A room link admits up to four players. Your hand is shared only with you and the host.
